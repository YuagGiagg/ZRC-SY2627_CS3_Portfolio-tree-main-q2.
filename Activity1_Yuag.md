2. If you were to add a new role called Intern, where would you place it in the inheritance
hierarchy? Why?
:I would place it under the Employee class in the inheritance hierarchy. Technically, an intern is a student, recent graduate, or trainee who works temporarily for an organization or hospital to gain supervised, practical experience in a specific field. That means they are legally considered employees. They are classified as temporary, part-time, or full-time employees.
